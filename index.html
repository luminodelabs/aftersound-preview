FAQs

Which songs can I promote?+

One released song on Spotify per promotion. Albums, playlists and unreleased songs are not supported yet.

Why do you ask for my audio if I paste a Spotify link?+

A Spotify link tells us which release to promote. It does not give us permission to use the recording, so you upload the audio you are authorized to use in ads.

Will this guarantee streams?+

No. We report clicks from your song page to Spotify. A click is not a confirmed stream, and no result is guaranteed.

Do I need my own TikTok ad account?+

Not in this demo. How ad accounts work for live promotions is still being decided. If one is needed, we will ask in setup and explain why.

What happens after I submit?+

Your promotion goes to review. It shows Submitted for review until delivery is confirmed, and only then shows Active.

Is aftersound ai part of TikTok or Spotify?+

No. aftersound ai is independent. We name TikTok and Spotify only to explain where your ads run and where listeners go.

Does this demo charge me or run ads?+

No. The demo runs in your browser. No ads will run and no payment will be taken.
